
Company Support FAQ

Shipping Policy
Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 5 business days.
Express shipping usually takes 1 to 2 business days.
Delivery estimates may vary by location.

Return Policy
Customers can request a return within 30 days of delivery.
Items must be unused and returned in their original packaging.
Some products may be excluded from the return policy.
Customers should contact support to initiate a return.

Damaged Products
Customers who receive a damaged product should contact support.
They should provide their order number and a description of the damage.
Photos of the damaged product may be requested.
Support will review the case and explain the available resolution.

Refund Policy
Refunds are processed after the returned item has been reviewed.
The time for the refund to appear depends on the payment provider.
The support team should confirm the applicable refund policy before promising a refund.

Contact Support
Customers can contact the support team for order issues,
returns, damaged products, and questions about company policies.
